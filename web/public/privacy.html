Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy covers Cricket Field Planner — the Android app and the web app at cricketfieldplanner.com. Both are built and maintained by an independent developer, not a company.

The short version

Nothing you enter in this app is collected, transmitted, or seen by the developer. Field layouts you save stay on your own device or in your own browser. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. The one exception — the optional AI Advice feature — sends data only to Google, only if you choose to use it, and only using an API key you provide yourself.

Data stored on your device

Custom field presets you save (the "Memory Slot" feature) are stored only locally:

  • On Android, in the app's private SharedPreferences storage.
  • On the web app, in your browser's localStorage.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data for cricketfieldplanner.com deletes it permanently. The developer has no access to it, no copy of it, and no way to recover it for you.

AI Advice (web app) — bring your own API key

The web app's "AI Advice" feature is optional and off by default. If you choose to use it, you provide your own Google Gemini API key, which is:

  • Stored only in your browser's localStorage — never sent to or stored by this app's developer.
  • Used to call Google's Generative Language API directly from your browser. There is no server in between. The only data sent in that request is the current field layout (fielder positions, match format, and phase) and your API key — no personal information, no account details, no browsing history.

That request, and Google's handling of it, is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API — not this developer's. You can remove your saved key at any time from within the app ("Change API Key").

The Android app does not yet have this feature (shown as "Coming Soon" in the app). When it ships, it will follow the same bring-your-own-key model, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

What this app does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in of any kind.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other device permissions requested.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age — there is nothing to collect.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices change (for example, when AI Advice ships on Android), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
